## How to read this record

This is a privacy-by-design record, not a declaration of legal compliance. RoastMyLP is the candidate Data Fiduciary/controller for customer-submitted and product-operational data. A vendor may be a processor for some data and an independent fiduciary/controller for other data. That classification, the lawful ground, statutory retention, international-transfer language, and the identity/contact details in the final notice require Indian privacy counsel review.

The DPDP Act and final Rules are implemented on staggered commencement dates. As of 2026-08-23, the product is designed toward the later operational duties, but this record does not claim that every rule is already in force or that early implementation alone proves compliance.

Retention below is a proposed maximum unless a longer legal hold or financial-record obligation is documented. `Delete` means removal from the active application system and capability revocation; provider backups, fraud records, mail delivery systems, and legally required payment records can follow different timelines disclosed below.

## Analytics and tracking prohibition

Phase 1 analytics is first-party, event-limited and operational:

- Allowed event names are server-owned enums such as `checkout_created`, `payment_captured`, `audit_completed`, `audit_failed`, `refund_terminal`, `email_delivered`, `scorecard_downloaded`, and `share_clicked`.
- No advertising ID, third-party analytics SDK, tracking pixel, browser fingerprint, cross-site identifier, customer segment, behavioral profile, remarketing audience, or sale/share of personal data is permitted.
- Resend open and click tracking must be disabled. A share click can be counted in the application before opening an external site; X or another social network is not contacted until the buyer acts.
- Private report pages load no third-party script, pixel, font, image or analytics resource and never emit capability tokens, full URLs, emails, payment IDs or report text.
- Sentry is error monitoring, not product analytics. Session Replay, user feedback widgets, Seer/AI features, default IP collection, and broad request-body/header capture remain disabled unless separately reviewed.

## Deletion and rights execution

1. Verify the requester without asking for excessive identity documents. Match a report capability plus a confirmation sent to the purchase email, or use a manually reviewed payment reference when the link is unavailable.
2. Revoke the capability token first so report access stops before asynchronous cleanup.
3. Mark a deletion tombstone, cancel unspent jobs, delete eligible active report/contact/operational data, and preserve only specifically documented financial/legal records.
4. Send processor deletion requests where the provider supports them. Record request date, outcome, expected backup expiry and any contract/legal exception.
5. Ensure restore procedures replay deletion tombstones so a backup cannot silently resurrect an erased report.
6. Tell the requester what was deleted, what must remain, why, and for how long. Do not claim immediate global erasure when backup, payment, mail or security retention remains.

Access, correction, erasure, consent withdrawal and grievance operations are implemented in POR-27/POR-30. This document supplies their data map; it does not mark those workflows complete.
