## Evidence standard

`Confirmed` means the linked public provider material currently states the fact. It does not prove the eventual RoastMyLP account has that setting, negotiated protection or region. `Configuration-dependent` requires dashboard/API evidence after the account exists. `Contract-required` requires an executed/incorporated DPA or written vendor response. Unverified marketing claims are not treated as controls.

## Cross-border and subprocessor review

Every production option currently exposes at least one cross-border or foreign-provider relationship. The final Privacy Notice must name provider categories and describe transfers accurately; counsel must decide the required contractual safeguards and notice/consent wording under Indian law and any foreign law triggered by customers or submitted-page content.

Before launch, capture a dated evidence pack for each production account:

1. Applicable service terms/DPA acceptance and plan.
2. Selected cloud/data region and residency controls.
3. Current subprocessor list/export and change-notification subscription.
4. Retention, backups, logs, tracking, model-data and deletion settings.
5. A test deletion/export result and any vendor support limitation.
6. Named owner and date for quarterly re-review and incident notifications.

## Required implementation controls derived from this review

- Provider adapters accept only the minimum typed data documented in the inventory.
- Raw IP, email, capability token, screenshot, report text, webhook body and provider secret are forbidden telemetry fields.
- QStash messages contain only opaque audit/job IDs; ScreenshotOne uses non-JSON binary output with cache/storage disabled; OpenAI uses `store:false` plus verified retention configuration.
- Resend tracking is off. Sentry pre-send processing uses an allowlist, not a best-effort denylist.
- A configuration manifest records provider plan, region, retention and policy-version evidence without storing credentials.
- Privacy Notice copy is generated only after the production account evidence pack is complete and counsel approves the role/retention/transfer statements.

## POR-11 conclusion

The inventory and public-source review are complete enough to guide implementation. None of the conditional/blocked providers is approved for live personal data merely by this ticket. POR-11 can close because uncertainty is explicitly recorded and routed to later launch blockers; live Razorpay credentials remain disabled until those risks have closure evidence.
